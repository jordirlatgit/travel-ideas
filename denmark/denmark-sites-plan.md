# Denmark Family Summer Itinerary (12 Days)

This itinerary is designed to minimize hotel changes while covering your Denmark sites with public transport-friendly day trips.

See [Denmark list of sites](denmark-sites.md) for detailed info for each site.

## Trip Structure (Hotel Bases)

- Base 1: Copenhagen area (Days 1-3)
- Base 2: Odense (Days 4-5)
- Base 3: Aarhus (Days 6-8)
- Base 4: Ribe (Days 9-10)
- Base 5: Nykobing Falster (Day 11)
- Final night/departure: Copenhagen area (Day 12)

## Day-by-Day Plan

### Day 1 - Arrive in Copenhagen, easy city day
- Morning/Afternoon: Arrival and hotel check-in.
- Late afternoon: Light walk in Copenhagen center and harbor area.
- Family pacing: Keep this day low intensity after travel.
- Sleep: Copenhagen area.

### Day 2 - Roskilde day trip
- Main visits: Viking Ship Museum, Roskilde Cathedral, fjord walk.
- Transport: Train Copenhagen -> Roskilde St, then short walk/local bus.
- Family tip: Add a playground or picnic break by the fjord.
- Sleep: Copenhagen area.

### Day 3 - Selandia highlights day trip
- Main visits (pick 2): Stevns Klint, Camp Adventure Forest Tower, Lejre Land of Legends.
- Transport: Regional train/S-train plus local buses (via Koge, Haslev, or Lejre depending on picks).
- Family tip: Do not try all 3 in one day with children; choose by weather and energy.
- Sleep: Copenhagen area.

### Day 4 - Transfer to Odense + Odense sights
- Morning: Train Copenhagen -> Odense St.
- Main visits: Hans Christian Andersen quarter, Munke Mose/Odense River area.
- Optional: Odense Zoo if arrival is early.
- Sleep: Odense.

### Day 5 - Billund and Vejle/Jelling day trip
- Main visits: LEGOLAND Billund or LEGO House, plus Vejle waterfront or Kongernes Jelling.
- Transport: Train to Vejle (or Give) + bus to Billund; continue by train/bus for Vejle/Jelling.
- Family tip: Choose either LEGOLAND or LEGO House as the anchor to avoid overload.
- Sleep: Odense.

### Day 6 - Transfer to Aarhus + city culture and nature
- Morning: Train Odense -> Aarhus H.
- Main visits: Den Gamle By, Marselisborg Deer Park (time permitting).
- Family tip: Keep evening flexible after transfer.
- Sleep: Aarhus.

### Day 7 - Grenaa day trip
- Main visits: Kattegatcentret, Grenaa Beach.
- Transport: Aarhus Letbanen/regional rail to Grenaa St + local bus/taxi.
- Family tip: Bring beach clothes and a wind layer.
- Sleep: Aarhus.

### Day 8 - Mols Bjerge and Himmelbjerget day choices
- Main visits: Nationalpark Mols Bjerge and/or Himmelbjerget (via Ry/Silkeborg).
- Transport: Regional bus to Mols Bjerge gateways (Ebeltoft/Knebel) or train to Ry/Silkeborg for Himmelbjerget.
- Family tip: Treat this as a choose-one-heavy-day or choose-two-light-stops day.
- Sleep: Aarhus.

### Day 9 - Long transfer to Ribe + old town
- Morning: Train Aarhus -> Ribe (usually with connection).
- Main visits: Ribe old town and evening riverside walk.
- Family tip: Keep this day simple because of transfer time.
- Sleep: Ribe.

### Day 10 - Wadden Sea and Mando day trip
- Main visits: Mando crossing, Wadden Sea mudflats, birdwatching/seal-focused guided activity.
- Transport: Ribe St -> Vester Vedsted by bus/taxi -> tractorbus to Mando (tide timetable).
- Family tip: Book guided tours in advance and check tide/weather windows.
- Sleep: Ribe.

### Day 11 - Transfer to Nykobing Falster + Nysted
- Morning: Train Ribe -> Nykobing F (long transfer with connections).
- Main visits: Nysted harbor and wetlands/coastal bird areas.
- Transport: Regional bus from Nykobing F St to Nysted.
- Family tip: Slower pace day works well after long rail travel.
- Sleep: Nykobing Falster.

### Day 12 - Mons Klint on route to Copenhagen, then departure
- Main visits: Mons Klint and GeoCenter (time permitting), then return toward Copenhagen for departure/last night.
- Transport: Bus/train via Vordingborg or Nykobing F routes, then onward rail to Copenhagen.
- Family tip: If flights/trains are early, move Mons Klint to Day 3 and keep Day 12 as pure return day.
- Sleep: Copenhagen area (or depart).

## Practical Notes

- Rubjerg Knude is not included in this 12-day public transport plan because it is remote and adds significant travel time.
- If Rubjerg Knude is a must, replace Day 11 with North Jutland (Hjorring/Lonstrup) and reduce Zealand stops.
- Use [Rejseplanen](https://www.rejseplanen.dk/webapp/?language=en_EN#!P|TP!histId|0!histKey|H145196) for real-time Danish public transport and tide-dependent timing for Mando.
- For family comfort, plan one lighter half-day every 3-4 days.
